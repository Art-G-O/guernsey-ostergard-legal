Safety Text Message Acknowledgment

Employee consent form · Guernsey-Ostergard Painting LLC · Last updated 18 November 2026

Guernsey-Ostergard Painting LLC sends its employees a text message each week with a link to that week's safety topic, so it can be read and acknowledged from a phone. Washington State requires us to hold safety meetings and to document who attended (WAC 296-155-110). This is how we do it.

We need your written permission before we text you. Please read this page and sign at the bottom.

Download a printable copy (PDF) — this is the form we hand out and keep on file.

What you will receive

  • The weekly safety topic. A link to the material you are required to read and acknowledge that week.
  • Notice when a safety rule changes. So that nobody meets a new requirement on a job site without having been told first.
  • A reminder if your acknowledgment has not come back yet.

We do not send marketing or promotional messages, and we will not use your number for anything else.

How often

Normally one message per week, plus the occasional reminder. Notices about a rule change are infrequent.

What it costs

Message and data rates may apply. Guernsey-Ostergard does not charge you for these messages. Any charge would come from your own mobile carrier under your own plan.

How to stop

Reply STOP to any message and we will send no more. You will get one confirmation that you have been unsubscribed. Reply START to begin again, or ask the office. Reply HELP at any time for contact details.

Stopping the texts does not affect your job. It does not remove the requirement to receive safety training and acknowledge it — the state requires that of both of us — so if you stop the texts we will give you the same material on paper and take your signature there instead. If you would rather have paper from the start, tell your foreman or the office. You do not have to sign up for texting first in order to ask for paper.

Your privacy

We record who was messaged, when, whether it was delivered, and the acknowledgment itself, for as long as our record-keeping obligations require. We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. The full detail is in our Privacy Policy, and the program terms are in our Terms and Conditions.
